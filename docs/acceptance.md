# Acceptance and thesis evidence boundary

Automated success is not the same as field acceptance. GABAY may be marked technically verified only after its unit, integration, static, migration, browser, offline, security, and packaging checks pass.

The 4 GB Pentium claim remains **unverified** until the Windows Lite acceptance script runs on the actual Lenovo. Target budgets are a 60-second cold start, 180 MB idle server working set, 450 MB forecast-worker peak, 600 MB combined GABAY process peak, two-second warm dashboard response, and a three-minute ten-year weekly experiment.

Participant sample size, the final usability instrument, statistical acceptance thresholds, real M'lang accuracy, and translation validation require adviser or stakeholder approval. Synthetic demo data never counts as research evidence.
